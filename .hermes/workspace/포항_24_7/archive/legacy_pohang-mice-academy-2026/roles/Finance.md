# Role: Finance

Responsibility
- Manage event budget, payments, and receipts.
- Prepare final financial report and reconciliations.

Key Deliverables
- Budget allocation sheet and payment approvals.
- Receipt records and final statement.

Authority
- Approve expenditures within small thresholds; escalate larger items to PM.

Backup
- PM

Initial Actions
- Create the budget tracker and allocate the remaining 369,000 KRW.
- Prepare vendor payment templates.

Success Metrics
- All receipts collected and final accounting closed within 2 weeks post-event.
