# Role: Operations Lead

Responsibility
- Venue logistics, AV, equipment rental, and on-site staffing.
- Run rehearsals and manage the event-day timeline.

Key Deliverables
- Equipment checklist and vendor contracts.
- Staffing plan and run sheet.
- Rehearsal sign-off.

Authority
- Approve operational vendors and minor onsite changes.

Backup
- Media Lead / Program Lead

Initial Actions
- Confirm venue equipment list; book rentals for missing items.
- Recruit and brief volunteers/staff.

Success Metrics
- Zero critical AV failures at event.
- Rehearsal completed with no schedule gaps.
