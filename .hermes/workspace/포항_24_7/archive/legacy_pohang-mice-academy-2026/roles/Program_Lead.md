# Role: Program Lead

Responsibility
- Design the event program, schedule, and judging criteria.
- Coordinate speaker/judge invitations and manage their briefings.

Key Deliverables
- Final program (detailed minute-by-minute schedule).
- Judge roster and scoring sheets.
- Presenter instructions and materials checklist.

Authority
- Approve final program flow and speaker lineup (subject to PM).

Backup
- Partner Coordinator / Operations Lead

Initial Actions (first week)
- Draft and circulate program template.
- Identify and approach potential judges.

Success Metrics
- Program finalized by D-45.
- Judges confirmed (>=3) by D-30.
