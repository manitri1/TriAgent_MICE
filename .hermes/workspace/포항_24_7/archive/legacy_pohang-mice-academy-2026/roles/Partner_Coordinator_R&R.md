# Partner Coordinator R&R


target: Partner Coordinator

purpose: Manage partner (university, tourism center, sponsors) outreach, MOU, and on-day partner logistics

responsibilities:
- Partner list & contact: maintain spreadsheet of partner orgs and contacts
- Outreach: send invitation, follow-ups, and coordinate cross-posting
- Sponsorship: manage sponsor proposal, benefits package, and deliverables
- Volunteer/partner desk: coordinate partner presence on event day
- Legal/document: collect MOU/partnership letters and share with PM/Finance

timeline:
- D-90: compile partner list and initial outreach
- D-60: confirm institutional partners (MOU) and in-kind support
- D-30: finalize sponsor benefits and space allocation
- D-Day: partner liaison desk and briefing
- D+14: sponsor/funder report and follow-up

deliverables:
- /opt/data/workspace/pohang-mice-academy-2026/partners/partner_list.csv
- sponsor proposal (template): /opt/data/workspace/pohang-mice-academy-2026/templates/sponsor_proposal_template.md
- MOU templates & confirmed letters

risks & mitigations:
- partner no-shows: secure written confirmation and 1 backup partner per slot
- sponsor withdrawal: tiered commitment with deposit where possible
- misaligned expectations: clear benefits matrix and activation timeline in sponsor agreement

operations:
- weekly outreach report to PM
- coordinate with Marketing for co-branded materials
