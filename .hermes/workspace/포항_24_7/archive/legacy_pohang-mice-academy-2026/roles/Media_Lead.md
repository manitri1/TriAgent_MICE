# Role: Media Lead

Responsibility
- Manage photography, video recording, and post-event media packages.
- Produce highlight video and photo gallery for promotion.

Key Deliverables
- Shooting plan and editing schedule.
- Delivery of photos and edited highlights within 2 weeks post-event.

Authority
- Hire freelance photographer/videographer within budget.

Backup
- Marketing Lead

Initial Actions
- Finalize shot list and confirm shooter availability.
- Define deliverables (formats, length, captions).

Success Metrics
- Photos and 1-2min highlight delivered within 14 days.
- Media used in at least 3 post-event promotions.
