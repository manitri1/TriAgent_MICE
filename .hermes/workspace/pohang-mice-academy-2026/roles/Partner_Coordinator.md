# Role: Partner & Volunteer Coordinator

Responsibility
- Liaise with partners (universities, tourism office) and recruit volunteers.
- Coordinate partner communications and on-site volunteer scheduling.

Key Deliverables
- Partner contact list and MOUs (if required).
- Volunteer roster and briefing pack.

Authority
- Confirm volunteer assignments and minor partner requests.

Backup
- Marketing Lead

Initial Actions
- Compile partner outreach list and send initial invites.
- Create volunteer signup and shift schedule.

Success Metrics
- Partners confirmed (>=3) by D-30.
- Volunteer coverage for all shifts (>=3 volunteers on duty).
