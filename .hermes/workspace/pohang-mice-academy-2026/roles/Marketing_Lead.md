# Role: Marketing Lead

Responsibility
- Plan and execute promotion to reach target attendees and submissions.
- Produce promotional materials (posters, SNS posts, email templates).

Key Deliverables
- Launch-ready registration form and campaign calendar.
- Creative assets (A3 posters, social cards, QR codes).
- Weekly registration performance reports.

Authority
- Approve creative assets and ad spend within budget.

Backup
- Media Lead

Initial Actions
- Publish registration form and first wave of SNS posts.
- Distribute A3 posters to campus and community hubs.

Success Metrics
- Registration: 50 participants (goal).
- Submission: 30 proposals.
