# Role: Project Manager (PM)

Responsibility
- Overall project leadership and final decision-maker.
- Liaise with host organizations, venue, sponsors, and local authorities.
- Approve budget allocations and sign off on major contracts.

Key Deliverables
- Project plan and milestone sign-offs.
- Weekly status reports to stakeholders.
- Final event readiness confirmation (rehearsal OK).

Authority
- Approve spend up to agreed limits.
- Confirm final program and key personnel.

Backup
- Operations Lead

Initial Actions (first week)
- Confirm venue booking & contact details.
- Finalize high-level timeline and milestone owners.

Success Metrics
- Program approved by D-30.
- Budget variance within +/- 10%.
