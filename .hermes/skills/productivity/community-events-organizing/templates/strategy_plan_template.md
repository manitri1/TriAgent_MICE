# Strategy Planning Template

## Project header
- Project name:
- Team name / lead:
- Date:

## 1. Executive summary (150-250 chars)
- "One-paragraph summary of what you want to achieve and why it matters."

## 2. Why (context & need)
- Provide local/contextual evidence: gap, user need, or opportunity this idea addresses.

## 3. Brand / project name & tagline
- Suggested name and 1-line tagline.

## 4. Objectives (SMART)
- Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound.
  - Input metric(s):
  - Activity metric(s):
  - Output metric(s):
  - Outcome/impact metric(s):

## 5. Target audience
- Primary & secondary audiences, rationale, and reach channels.

## 6. Positioning & guiding principles
- How is this idea different? What are priority constraints (cost, accessibility)?

## 7. Expected impact (short/medium/long)
- 0-3 months, 3-12 months, 1+ year expectations.

## 8. Implementation plan (How)
- Channels & tactics (marketing, partners), operations, resources needed.

## 9. Budget summary (high level)
- Top-level cost buckets and estimate.

## 10. Risks & mitigations
- Top 3 risks and how you'll address them.

## 11. Timeline & milestones
- Key milestones and dates from now to D+X.

## 12. Deliverables & owners
- List of deliverables and assigned owner + backup.

## 13. Appendices (attachments)
- Links to slides, proposal PDF, images, sample scripts.

---

Use this template as a copyable markdown for team submissions. Keep each section concise (use bullet points). The reviewing panel will look for clarity on Why/How/Impact and a realistic budget & timeline.
