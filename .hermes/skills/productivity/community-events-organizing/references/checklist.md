# Day-of checklist — Showcase / Small contest

Pre-event (D-1)
- [ ] Final participant list confirmed and contact numbers collected
- [ ] Slides/PDF for each presenter received and backed up (USB + cloud)
- [ ] AV check: projector, HDMI, laptop, wireless mic, spare batteries
- [ ] Seating set per layout_no_booth.svg; aisles cleared
- [ ] Registration desk set (staff assigned), name-tags printed
- [ ] Photographer/videographer briefed and consent forms ready

Hour-of
- [ ] Registration opens (recommended 30 min before start)
- [ ] Presenter check-in and rehearsal schedule announced
- [ ] MC / host confirms cues with AV operator
- [ ] Timekeeper assigned (visible countdown)

Post-event
- [ ] Collect presenter feedback & upload photos/video to assets/
- [ ] Collect receipts and submit to Finance
- [ ] Send thank-you + survey email to participants
- [ ] Produce highlight clip (1-2 min) and distribute

Notes
- Keep an "emergency kit": extra laptop, HDMI adapter, 2x wireless lapel mics, power strips, gaffer tape.
- For small contests, limit live presenters to 8 teams to keep schedule tight.
