Booth assessment & small-showcase guidance

Context: single-room indoor contest (example: Pohang content contest), ~50 participants, stage presentations primary.

Decision summary
- Default: NO full exhibition booths for single-room, 50-person pitch/contest events. Booths add footprint, staffing, noise, and risk to presentation flow.
- Preferred alternative: Showcase Corner (2–4 demo tables or poster boards) + Digital Showcase (slide loop / QR gallery).

Checklist to run with stakeholders
1. Purpose: Is the goal to (A) let multiple vendors sell/show products, or (B) show a few selected projects? If A → booth may be required. If B → Showcase Corner.
2. Space: Measure usable floor area and clearances (seating + stage + aisle). Can you spare 2m x 6m for booths without reducing seating below target? If NO → avoid booths.
3. Budget: Can you fund booths, furniture, power, and extra staff within remaining budget? Small booth line-item: table + chair + banner ~20k–60k KRW each.
4. Staffing: Does each booth have at least 1 dedicated staff/volunteer during event to avoid distracting presenters? If NO → avoid full booths.
5. AV & Power: Do booths need power/monitor? Confirm available circuits and cable routing safety.
6. Circulation: Will booths block presenter Q&A or audience sightlines? If YES → avoid.
7. Safety/Fire egress: Check local fire code and keep min aisle widths.

Recommended small setups
- Showcase Corner (recommended)
  - Location: rear wall or side wall, away from stage sightlines and main aisle.
  - Capacity: 2–4 demo tables (table 120cm), each with 1 chair and small banner or easel.
  - Power: 1 shared outlet per 2 tables; encourage battery-powered demos when possible.
  - Booking: Invitation-only or selected from submissions (no fee).

- Poster / Easel Wall
  - Use 3–6 easels for printed posters or A2/A1 boards along wall.
  - Low cost, static, no power needed.

- Digital Showcase (low-effort high-impact)
  - Slide loop on side screen with 30–60s project highlights.
  - QR gallery: each submission has QR linking to proposal, video, or images.
  - Advantages: no staffing, minimal footprint, scalable.

If you must run booths (not recommended for single-room 50p)
- Layout: single row along the back wall (face in) or two rows in back-to-back cluster behind seating.
- Booth spec (minimum): 120cm table, 1 chair, 1 power outlet, small footprint banner.
- Spacing: allow 1.2–1.5m aisle in front of row for circulation.
- Contracts: require vendor insurance, itemized quote, and contact person.

Operational checklist for Showcase Corner
- Confirm location and measure dimensions
- Reserve tables, easels, power strips
- Create booking form (name, contact, size of display, need power)
- Assign one staff to oversee Showcase Corner during the event
- Add small signage: "Showcase Corner — do not obstruct stage"

Budget notes (rough)
- Table rental: 10–30k KRW each
- Easel/panel rental: 5–20k KRW each
- Simple banner/printing: 10–40k KRW
- Digital slide loop: free if on existing laptop/monitor

When to say NO to booths (short list)
- Venue single-room, seating tight for target attendance
- Budget insufficient to support rentals and staffing
- Organizers want minimal logistical complexity
