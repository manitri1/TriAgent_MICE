Marketing checklist & templates (for local events)

Purpose: session-specific, reusable marketing assets and a week-by-week TODO template to support small-to-medium events (idea contests, workshops, meetups).

Where to save outputs (convention)
- Project folder: ./proposals/<project-slug>/marketing/
- Example in this session: /opt/data/workspace/pohang-mice-academy-2026/marketing/

Week-by-week TODO (copy & adapt)
- Week 1: setup registration form (Google Forms), draft A3 poster + SNS card copy, define channels and KPIs
- Week 2: finalize poster artwork, prepare 2 SNS variants (1080×1080), create QR/shortlink
- Week 3: launch initial post & partner outreach, test form uploads and notification flow
- Week 4: collect partner repost agreements, adjust copy for each channel, run first push to campuses
- Week 5: mid-campaign check (CTR, conversion), prepare FAQ and response templates
- Week 6: collect speaker/participant bios, publish reminder posts, confirm venue promotion
- Week 7: schedule paid/boosted posts if needed, finalize print materials distribution
- Week 8: confirm on-site digital check-in flow (tablets/QR), finalize day-of content plan
- Week 9: D-14 detailed schedule post, rehearsal of live streams and content capture
- Week 10: D-7 final checks, publish ‘last call’ posts, prepare team roles for on-site
- D-1 to D-Day: live posting plan, on-site registration support, monitoring form submissions
- D+1–D+14: post-event assets (photo/video), KPI report, lessons learned

Promo copy templates
- Poster headline (A3): “포항의 새로운 매력을 발견하세요 — 포항 지역 콘텐츠 발굴 콘테스트”
- Poster body: date/time, location, 참가대상, 신청방법(QR), 문의처(PM 연락처)
- SNS short caption (generic): “2026.11.03 18:00 | 파랑뜰 2층 — 포항 지역 콘텐츠 발굴 콘테스트. 지금 신청 ▶ [링크/QR] #포항 #마이스아카데미”
- Instagram long caption: hook (1–2 lines) + 한줄 요약 + 참가방법 + 해시태그(3–5개) + CTA link

Canva prompt (example)
- "Clean modern poster for a local idea contest in Pohang. Use ocean-blue and coral accent colors, bold Korean headline: '포항의 새로운 매력을 발견하세요'. Include date 2026.11.03, time 18:00, location '파랑뜰 2층', and a prominent CTA area for registration link/QR. Provide 2 variants: (1) photo background of Pohang coastline with semi-transparent overlay, (2) flat geometric illustration with iconography for travel and ideas. Include sponsor logo area at bottom."
